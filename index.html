My Story

I'm an Electrical Power & Renewable Energy Engineer with an Erasmus Mundus Joint Master's in Energy Systems Optimization. My journey has taken me across four European universities — L'Aquila, Vienna, Barcelona, and Brussels — building a strong technical foundation in power systems modelling, renewable integration, and energy data science.

During my internship at ENTSO-E, I applied machine learning to time-series forecasting on Germany, Belgium, and the Netherlands 2024 hourly load data and transformed raw data from 172 energy meters into strategic business intelligence. My work at UNDP involved managing 10 international solar PV installations, where I used HOMER Pro and PV*SOL to reduce LCOE by 15% and increase renewable fraction by 40%.

I'm now focused on becoming an Energy Analyst and Electricity Grid Modeller, applying my engineering expertise and data analytics skills to accelerate Europe's zero-carbon energy transition.
