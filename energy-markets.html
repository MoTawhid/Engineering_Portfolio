Energy Markets

My thesis research on electricity markets and load behaviour — together with the interactive dashboards built from it.

🎓 Master's Thesis — Energy Markets

My thesis investigates electricity market dynamics and short-term load behaviour across European bidding zones, combining time-series analysis with machine-learning forecasting on ENTSO-E Transparency Platform data.

🎯

Research Question

How do market signals and load patterns interact across coupled European bidding zones?

🛠️

Methodology

Time-series analysis · Machine learning forecasting · Market data analytics

🗂️

Data Sources

ENTSO-E Transparency Platform · ERAA dataset · Historical hourly load

🏛️

Institution

Erasmus Mundus Joint Master — Energy Systems Optimization

📄 💻

Key Contributions

📈

Load Forecasting Pipeline

Hourly electricity load forecasting benchmarked across classical time-series and machine-learning models.

🔍

Market Data Exploration

Structured analysis of cross-border flows, generation mix, and adequacy metrics across European TSOs.

🧪

Reproducible Tooling

Research packaged as two public, interactive Streamlit applications for non-technical stakeholders.

🌍

Policy Relevance

Findings translated into actionable insights for grid stability and capacity planning.
